Test: Correct Focus Indicators for Non-Rectangular Buttons

These buttons use appropriate focus indicators that follow their shapes, ensuring the focus indicator stays connected to the button even at high magnification.

Circular Button (border-radius) with Box-Shadow

Circle made with border-radius (not clip-path), so the box-shadow ring is not clipped and follows the circular shape.

Diamond Button (rotated square) with Box-Shadow

Diamond made by rotating a square 45° (not clip-path), so the box-shadow ring rotates with the element and follows the diamond. The label is counter-rotated to stay upright.

Rounded Rectangle with Outline (Acceptable)

Border-radius creates soft corners - outline follows reasonably well (no severe mismatch).

Regular Rectangle with Outline (Optimal)

Standard rectangular button with outline - the recommended approach.

Best Practice: A focus indicator must follow the button's actual shape. clip-path clips outline, box-shadow, border and even filter on the same element, so a clipped element cannot carry a shape-following focus ring. Build non-rectangular buttons with border-radius (circle) or transform: rotate() (diamond) instead — the ring is then part of the transformed element and follows the shape.
