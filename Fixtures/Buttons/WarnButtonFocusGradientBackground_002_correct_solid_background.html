Test: Solid Background Buttons

These buttons have solid colour backgrounds. The focus outline uses a positive outline-offset, so it is drawn in the gap just outside the button, over the container's solid background. Because that background is a known solid colour, the focus-outline contrast (WCAG 1.4.11) can be verified automatically.

Dark Outline on Light Container

Dark blue outline (#0066cc) sits over the white container background - 5.6:1 contrast.

Light Outline on Dark Container

Light blue outline (#66b3ff) sits over the dark container background - 7.4:1 contrast.

White Outline on Blue Container

White outline (#ffffff) sits over the blue container background - 7.8:1 contrast. The focus ring lands on the blue area, not the white page, so it stays visible.
