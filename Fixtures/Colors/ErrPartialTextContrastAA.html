Error: Partial Text Contrast - Some Text Cannot Be Tested

Purpose: Each case FAILS contrast (< 4.5:1) for text inside a solid container, AND some of that text extends outside the container onto an undefined background. Result: ErrPartialTextContrastAA. The two gradient/image cases cannot have their inside contrast computed at all, so they are reported as WarnTextContrastCannotCalculate instead.

Section 1: Horizontal Overflow with Failing Contrast Inside

Detection: Text inside fails contrast AND content extends beyond the box horizontally.

PARTIAL-1: Horizontal Overflow (nowrap) - Failing Inside
Light gray on gray inside (fails 1.77:1), and this text extends beyond the gray container with no background.
✗ Text inside FAILS contrast. ⚠ Text outside cannot be tested. Result: ErrPartialTextContrastAA.

Section 2: Vertical Overflow with Failing Contrast Inside

Detection: Text inside fails contrast AND content extends below the box.

PARTIAL-2: Vertical Overflow - Failing Inside
Fails inside (1.53:1).
Line 2 overflows.
Line 3 overflows.
Line 4 overflows.
✗ Text inside FAILS contrast. ⚠ Lines 2-4 overflow, cannot be tested. Result: ErrPartialTextContrastAA.

Section 3: Absolute Positioning with Failing Contrast Inside

Detection: Text inside fails contrast AND nowrap content overflows the absolutely-positioned box.

PARTIAL-3: Absolute Positioning - Failing Inside
Medium gray on dark gray fails (1.57:1) and extends beyond its coloured box; the absolutely-positioned element uses nowrap so the text runs past its background.
✗ Text inside FAILS contrast. ⚠ Overflowing text cannot be tested. Result: ErrPartialTextContrastAA.

Section 4: Negative Margin with Failing Contrast Inside

Detection: Text inside fails contrast AND negative margin pushes the box outside its parent.

PARTIAL-4: Negative Margin - Failing Inside
Medium gray on dark gray fails (1.62:1) with negative left margin. Part extends left with no background.
✗ Text inside FAILS contrast. ⚠ Text in negative margin cannot be tested. Result: ErrPartialTextContrastAA.

Section 5: Transform Translation with Failing Contrast Inside

Detection: Text inside fails contrast AND transform pushes the box outside its parent.

PARTIAL-5: Transform Translation - Failing Inside
Medium gray on dark gray fails (1.66:1) with transform translateX. Part extends left with no background.
✗ Text inside FAILS contrast. ⚠ Transformed text outside cannot be tested. Result: ErrPartialTextContrastAA.

Section 6: Rotated Text with Failing Contrast Inside

Detection: Text inside fails contrast AND nowrap content overflows the box (which is also rotated).

PARTIAL-6: Rotated Text - Failing Inside
Light gray on medium gray fails (1.53:1); the nowrap text runs past the coloured box, which is rotated -5deg.
✗ Text inside FAILS contrast. ⚠ Overflowing/rotated text outside cannot be tested. Result: ErrPartialTextContrastAA.

Section 7: Floated Element with Failing Contrast Inside

Detection: Text inside fails contrast AND nowrap content overflows the narrow floated box.

PARTIAL-7: Floated Element - Failing Inside
Medium gray on dark gray fails (1.57:1); the nowrap text runs past this narrow floated box.
✗ Text inside FAILS contrast. ⚠ Overflowing text outside cannot be tested. Result: ErrPartialTextContrastAA.

Section 8: Overflow on Gradient (Cannot Compute Inside Contrast)

Detection: Gradient background means the inside contrast cannot be computed, so this is a warning, not a partial-contrast error.

PARTIAL-8: Overflow on Gradient - Cannot Calculate
Light gray on a gray gradient - the contrast cannot be computed automatically, and text also extends beyond the container.
⚠ Gradient background: inside contrast cannot be computed. Result: WarnTextContrastCannotCalculate.

Section 9: Overflow on Image (Cannot Compute Inside Contrast)

Detection: Background image means the inside contrast cannot be computed, so this is a warning, not a partial-contrast error.

PARTIAL-9: Overflow on Image - Cannot Calculate
Light gray on a gray SVG image - the contrast cannot be computed automatically, and text also extends beyond the container.
⚠ Background image: inside contrast cannot be computed. Result: WarnTextContrastCannotCalculate.

Section 10: Transparent Background with Overflow and Failing Contrast

Detection: Transparent element resolves to its solid parent background (failing), AND nowrap content overflows.

PARTIAL-10: Transparent with Overflow - Failing Inside
Light gray on transparent (gray parent) fails (2.85:1), and the nowrap text extends beyond the parent onto a different background.
✗ Text inside FAILS contrast on parent. ⚠ Overflow text on unknown background cannot be tested. Result: ErrPartialTextContrastAA.

Section 11: Animation with Overflow and Failing Contrast

Detection: Text inside fails contrast AND nowrap content overflows the animated coloured box.

PARTIAL-11: Animated Overflow - Failing Inside
Medium gray on dark gray fails (1.66:1); the nowrap text runs past the coloured box, which also animates across the page.
✗ Text inside FAILS contrast. ⚠ Overflowing/animated text outside cannot be tested. Result: ErrPartialTextContrastAA.

Summary: Sections 1-7, 10 and 11 are partial-contrast errors: the text fails contrast inside a solid container (< 4.5:1) and overflows it, so part of the text sits on an undefined background. Sections 8 and 9 use a gradient/image background whose inside contrast cannot be computed, so they are warnings (WarnTextContrastCannotCalculate) rather than partial-contrast errors. Each case is validated individually through its data-expected-* annotation.
